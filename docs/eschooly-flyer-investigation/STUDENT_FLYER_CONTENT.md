# Flyer A — Eschooly Student App

**Use only the “Confirmed for promotion” block on the A4 flyer.**  
Do not add features from the excluded list. Do not invent testimonials, uptime, or school counts.

**Accuracy note for the designer (not for print):** These features are implemented in the student app and connected to the school API on the device. Live school data was not independently tested in this investigation. If the flyer must say “live”, obtain a runtime sign-off first. Safe print language is “in the Student app” / “from your school records” without “real-time guaranteed”.

---

## Headline

**Your school day, in one student app.**

Alternatives:

- **Classes, homework, and results — with you.**
- **Stay on top of school, from home screen to report card.**

## Introductory description

Eschooly Student gives learners a single place to see their timetable, attendance, assignments, exams, results, library resources, and online classes. Sign in with a student ID, then use only the tools your school has switched on.

## Key features (confirmed for promotion)

Promote these. Short copy is sized for an A4 column.

| Feature | Flyer explanation | Suggested visual / icon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student home | Start with a greeting, your class, and shortcuts your school has enabled. | Phone home dashboard; student with backpack |
| Timetable | Check each day’s periods, subjects, and teachers. | Weekly schedule / clock |
| Attendance | See monthly presence, absences, and history. | Calendar with check marks |
| Assignments | Open homework by subject, add notes, attach files, and submit. | Notebook and checklist |
| Exams | View exam overview, the next paper, and past results. | Exam paper / clipboard |
| Results | Read subject grades and open your result card. | Report card / trophy |
| Syllabus | Follow units and topics for each subject. | Open textbook / progress list |
| Digital library | Search class resources and open files on your phone. | Stack of books |
| Online classes | Join live lessons, set a reminder, and replay recordings when provided. | Laptop / video class |
| Announcements | Read school notices in one list. | Megaphone |
| Notifications | Open alerts and mark them as read. | Bell |
| Holidays | See upcoming school holidays. | Calendar with sun |
| Profile | View your details, change your password, and sign out. | Student ID / profile |

**Teachers directory** may be mentioned as “see your class and subject teachers” only if the designer has space. Do **not** claim in-app teacher messaging as a live service (chat is local demo UI).

## Main student benefits

- One login for timetable, work, and results  
- Homework you can submit from the phone  
- Library files and online lessons without a separate portal  
- Clear attendance and exam history  
- School notices and alerts in the same app  

## Optional combined line

“Built for students who need their timetable, assignments, and results in one place.”

## Do not print (excluded)

- Live chat with teachers or school admin  
- Sitting a full online exam / midterm inside the app  
- Fee payment, challans, or dues  
- Transport or bus tracking  
- “Report card ready” push as a separate feature  
- Any performance statistic, award, or testimonial  

## Tone

Professional, calm, school-appropriate. Prefer “view”, “submit”, “join”, “download” over “AI”, “smart”, or “all-in-one super app”.
